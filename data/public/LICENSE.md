quad_cities.json.gz contains roads and mapped facilities from the Iowa and
Illinois Geofabrik extracts dated 2026-10-23, © OpenStreetMap contributors,
under ODbL 1.0. Mapped facility records link to their source OSM objects.
Facility availability, services and allocation of resources are not verified.
Quad Cities city boundaries and tract population are official Census 2020
public data. Original source URLs, retrieval times and SHA-256 hashes are in
the snapshot and docs/quad_cities_manifest.json. The public snapshot needs
no raw downloads at runtime.
